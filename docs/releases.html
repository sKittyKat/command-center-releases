Release notes

Every version, newest first. The installers and their SHA-256 checksums are on the releases page; the app tells you when a new one is out.

0.2.2 Latest

November 16, 2026 · Download

  • Claude Code, found everywhere. With Claude paid by your plan, Check looks everywhere Claude Code gets installed on a Mac and on Windows, including the copy inside the Claude desktop app, and uses the one signed in with your plan.
  • Exact words to sign in. If the only copy isn't on your PATH, Check tells you exactly what to type in a terminal.

0.2.1

November 16, 2026 · Download

  • Settings in tabs: Claude, Touchpoints, Sources and Your workspace, one at a time.
  • Long lists folded behind Show all: Notion's databases and pages, Slack channels, QuickBooks clients, the week's phone alerts.
  • More motion: the next touchpoint's mark breathes; buttons, rows and icons answer when you point at them; a marker glides under the tab you pick; a row you approve slides out before the rest close up; checks, counts and ticks pop in. Everything stays still if your computer is set to reduce motion.

0.2.0

November 16, 2026 · Download

The first release:

  • Today and the agenda: Approve, Review, Reply and Decide, with keys, snooze, pin, delegate and the command bar.
  • Touchpoints: the morning brief, midday sweep and end-of-day wrap, the Monday plan and Friday review, phone alerts and the audio brief.
  • The inbox: Gmail and Slack, free rules then Claude, drafts in your voice, the 24-hour watch.
  • Projects and clients with health and slips; site agents with a QA gate.
  • Money: QuickBooks and Stripe, invoices from milestones, the Friday reconcile, flags, personal statements; accounts and cards.
  • The Notion import, backups and restore, and the update check.
  • A first-launch set-up that walks you through all of it.
