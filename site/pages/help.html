Troubleshooting

The common snags, and what to do about each. Everything Claude and you did is in the action log, with Undo where it can have one, and uninstalling never touches your data.

The Mac won't open the app

The app isn't signed yet, so macOS asks once. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Command Center and choose Open Anyway. On macOS 14 or earlier, right-click the app and choose Open.

Windows says it protected your PC

In the SmartScreen notice, choose More info, then Run anyway. It's the same unsigned-app check, once.

No notifications on the Mac

Allow them in System Settings › Notifications › Command Center. Settings › Touchpoints says when the system didn't show the last one. Your briefs still land on Today and in the menu bar.

Command Center can't find Claude Code

With Claude paid by your plan, Check looks everywhere Claude Code gets installed, including the copy inside the Claude desktop app. If it still finds none, install Claude Code from claude.ai/install, then choose Check again. You don't need to restart Command Center.

Claude Code isn't signed in

Check says exactly what to type in a terminal: claude, or the full path of the copy it found (for the desktop app's copy). Run it, sign in with your Claude account (the one with your plan), then choose Check again. If it's signed in with something other than a Claude plan, its use is billed to that sign-in, and Settings says so.

Google won't let me choose Internal

Internal is for Google Workspace accounts. With a personal Gmail, choose External, keep it in testing, and add yourself as a test user. Google then asks you to sign in again every 7 days.

Slack says it's missing scopes for channels

Add channels:read, channels:history, groups:read and groups:history, and the events message.channels and message.groups. Then Reinstall to Workspace. If Slack shows a new User OAuth Token, disconnect Slack in Command Center and connect again with it.

Claude stopped sorting

Settings › Sources › Inbox says why: the monthly cap reached, no credit left on your Anthropic account, or a key this computer can't open (save it again on the Claude card, in Settings › Claude). The free rules keep filing in the meantime.

QuickBooks sign-in didn't finish

Paste back the whole address your browser lands on after you pick your company, within 15 minutes. It works once: if it timed out, choose Sign in to QuickBooks again.

Stripe refused my key

Command Center only accepts a restricted key with read access to the balance, payments and payouts. A full secret key (sk_…) is refused on purpose.

A restore asks for a passphrase

The backup was sealed with a key this computer doesn't hold. Type the passphrase you set backups up with.

No phone alerts

Check that your phone's ntfy app is subscribed to the exact topic Settings shows, then choose Send a test. Alerts don't go during quiet hours (9 PM to 7 AM to start), while the touchpoints are paused, or past the daily limit.

Something else

The action log has every change, with Undo where it can have one. Your data folder is never touched by uninstalling, so a reinstall picks up where you left off.
