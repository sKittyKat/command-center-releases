Release notes

Every version, newest first. The installers and their SHA-256 checksums are on the releases page; the app tells you when a new one is out.

0.2.3 Latest

November 19, 2026 · Download

  • Notion, without its old limits. Big workspaces (up to 20,000 pages), relations, rollups and formulas read whole, and people by name.
  • Contracts and accounts come in from Notion too; a contract's file is copied in. Passwords and card numbers are never read.
  • Changes in Notion after you import show field by field: bring them in, or keep yours.
  • Locking ticks itself: the retire checklist sees when your databases are locked in Notion.
  • A wider Notion import card in Settings, so each page's buttons and changes have room.

See Bringing in Notion.

0.2.1

November 16, 2026 · Download

  • Settings in tabs: Claude, Touchpoints, Sources and Your workspace, one at a time.
  • Long lists folded behind Show all: Notion's databases and pages, Slack channels, QuickBooks clients, the week's phone alerts.
  • More motion: the next touchpoint's mark breathes; buttons, rows and icons answer when you point at them; a marker glides under the tab you pick; a row you approve slides out before the rest close up; checks, counts and ticks pop in.
